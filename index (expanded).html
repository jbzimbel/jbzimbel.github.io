Jacob Bradley Zimbelman

Doctoral Candidate & Instructor in Philosophy

Saint Louis University

I work mainly at the intersection of epistemology, practical ethics, and political theory. My dissertation is on the rationality of political suspicion. I'm also currently interested in what implications the rise of AI in mass media and in governance might have for the norms of political trust and suspicion.

At SLU, I've taught over 400 students across 11 semesters. I mainly teach two courses—Ultimate Questions: The Examined Life and Ethics—which are core requirements for most students. It's a very cool job.

In my spare time, I'm also a musician, sound engineer, and backpacker.

AOS: Epistemology, Ethics, Political/Social AOC: Religion, Mind, Technology, History
